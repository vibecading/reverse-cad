These are design drawings, not a survey of the built house.

## Read from the drawings (P)

Wing on the south end of the east front: 25'-0" wide on the plan; on the east
elevation the wall scales to 25'-10" at the vertical scale of the dimensioned
storey heights (24.63 px/ft on the 3840 px sheet), so plan and elevation
disagree by 3 %. Gable overhang beyond the side walls 5'-10" (dimensioned).
Scaled from the elevation, from the bottom of the shingles: top of the verge
at the apex 7.33 m, at its lower ends 5.67 m; window casings (outer lines,
including head casing and sill board) 2nd floor 3.87-5.63 m, 1st floor
0.74-2.48 m.
