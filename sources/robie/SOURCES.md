## Scale of the scans

The sheets are not square: on sheet 2 the 161'-6" string gives 19.98 px/ft
across the sheet and the 46'-6" string 19.66 px/ft down it (paper or scanner
stretch, 1.6 %). The scale bars on sheets 2, 5 and 6 are horizontal and give
20.0 px/ft. Lengths read across a sheet use 19.98, lengths read down it 19.66.

## Read from the drawings (P), all scaled, none dimensioned

Ground-floor west enclosure (the brick wall under the living-room porch),
origin at its outer south-west corner:
- west face 22'-4" (sheet 2, 439 px down the sheet), 22'-5" on the west elevation (sheet 6, 449 px across): 6.85 m;
- south face to the west face of the stair-wall jog 13'-2" (sheet 2): 4.01 m; the south elevation (sheet 5) shows 12'-6" to the step;
- coping top 8'-5", coping underside 7'-9", bottom of brick 1'-0" above grade (sheet 6): 2.56, 2.35, 0.31 m.

Living-room roof, top of the fascia 16'-1" above grade (sheet 6): 4.91 m.
Its west edge is 5'-9" east of the enclosure's west face on sheet 4 and 6'-3"
on sheet 5. North and south edges: sheet 4 puts them 4'-5" beyond the
enclosure's north face and 1'-10" beyond its south face, the west elevation
3'-1" and 2'-8". The two sheets disagree by about 0.3 m on the roof.
